# Jeju PixelFlip Pre

## Project Positioning

Jeju PixelFlip Pre is a prepared, explorable visual presentation prototype about travel in Jeju Island, South Korea. It is designed as a speaker-led presentation system built around handcrafted pixel-art worlds, layered interaction hotspots, and a structured page graph.

The project is not a conventional tourism website, not a slide deck in the usual PPT sense, and not a live AI generation product. Its purpose is to present curated travel knowledge through a navigable visual atlas that feels immersive, spatial, and presentation-ready.

## Target Experience

The audience opens on a 16:9 pixel-art overview of Jeju Island that feels like a miniature explorable world. Instead of reading bullet-heavy slides, they are invited to understand the island through place, terrain, routes, landmarks, food culture, and seasonal logic.

The speaker drives the experience by clicking visual regions to branch into deeper prepared pages. Each click should feel like zooming into a meaningful chapter of the island rather than advancing to the next flat slide.

Core experience goals:

- Make the presentation feel like navigating a knowledge map.
- Make each page feel like a polished pixel diorama, not a screenshot from a game.
- Support speaker storytelling without requiring live content generation.
- Keep information legible and modular by separating visuals from text overlays.
- Balance charm and professionalism so the prototype can be presented in product, design, or travel contexts.

## Target Users

- Designers exploring new presentation formats
- Founders or creative teams pitching an experience concept
- Speakers presenting travel research in a memorable format
- Audiences who respond better to spatial and visual storytelling than linear decks

## How It Differs From PPT

Traditional PPT is linear, text-led, and page-sequential. Jeju PixelFlip Pre is spatial, visual-first, and selectively branching.

Key differences:

- The primary canvas is an explorable map world rather than a stack of slides.
- Navigation is based on hotspots and page relationships, not only next/previous flow.
- The background scene carries narrative context before any text is read.
- Detail pages act like zoom-ins or subregions of one world, not disconnected slides.
- The speaker can adapt the path live while staying within a prepared content system.

## How It Differs From Real-Time Flipbook Systems

Real-time Flipbook-style systems generate or reshape visuals and content dynamically. Jeju PixelFlip Pre deliberately does not.

Key differences:

- All content is prepared in advance and manually reviewed.
- No live AI image generation during the presentation.
- No dependency on prompt quality during delivery.
- Visuals, hotspots, and page relationships are controlled and predictable.
- Text remains editable and maintainable outside the image layer.

## Delivery Principles

- Prepared, not generated live
- Exploratory, not linear-only
- Visually rich, not text-heavy
- Pixel-crafted, not retro-for-retro's-sake
- Presentation-grade, not game UI cosplay
