# Scene Card: ext-jusangjeolli

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Core Sight Map. This page turns the coastal cliff anchor into a readable geological close-up.

## Core Point

Jusangjeolli makes Jeju's volcanic history visible as form.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should see the basalt columns as a natural architecture shaped by lava, cooling, sea impact, and time.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel-art cliff-edge world with strong hexagonal basalt-column rhythm, white surf, dark volcanic stone, viewing path cues, and a clear sea-edge composition.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 isometric/angled coastal section. The basalt columns should dominate the lower or side edge, with ocean movement and cliff geometry clearly separated. Leave a calm sky/ocean zone for UI overlay.

## Must Include

- Repeating basalt column geometry
- Dark volcanic rock and bright white surf
- Cliff edge, viewing platform/path, or tiny visitors for scale
- Coastal water with wave impact
- Clear distinction between stone structure and ocean
- Hotspot-ready zones for columns, surf edge, and viewpoint

## Avoid

- Generic gray cliff without column structure
- Fantasy canyon or monumental castle-like columns
- Photorealistic geology diagram
- Text labels, arrows, UI overlays, or explanatory captions baked into art
- Overly dark mood that reduces presentation readability

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain basalt columns, wave erosion, and viewpoint safety.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve upper-right sky/ocean or a low-detail sea area. Do not place the column rhythm under the content panel.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art close-up of Jusangjeolli Cliffs in Jeju, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with the Jeju PixelFlip world, dramatic but readable basalt column coastline, repeating hexagonal volcanic rock forms, white surf hitting dark stone, small viewing path and tiny visitors for scale, bright coastal water, polished professional presentation background, clear geological structure, calm right-side UI safe zone, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Generic cliff, fantasy canyon, castle columns, photorealistic geology diagram, dark horror mood, text labels, arrows, UI panels, tourism poster, crude 8-bit art, blurry painterly rocks, overcrowded visitors.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Basalt-column identity is clear at presentation distance
- Feels like a closer view of the coastal/geology anchor from the parent map
- UI panel does not cover the key column geometry
- The scene can support a 30-60 second explanation of volcanic form
