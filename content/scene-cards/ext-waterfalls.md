# Scene Card: ext-waterfalls

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Core Sight Map. This page adds Jeju's lush vertical-water landscape to balance the volcanic coast and mountain scenes.

## Core Point

Jeju's waterfalls reveal a softer, wetter side of the volcanic island.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand that Jeju is not only basalt and beaches; it also has subtropical greenery, ravines, mist, and scenic water movement.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel-art waterfall world with vertical white water, dark basalt walls, dense green foliage, mist, small viewing paths, and tiny visitors.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 angled ravine/coast composition. Keep the waterfall form strong and vertical, with surrounding greenery and rock texture framing it. Leave safe UI space over calmer sky, water, or low-detail foliage.

## Must Include

- One or more clear waterfall streams
- Dark volcanic rock or basalt ravine walls
- Dense subtropical greenery
- Mist, pool, or flowing water cues
- Viewing path, bridge, or tiny visitors for scale
- Hotspot-ready zones for waterfall, viewpoint, and surrounding landscape

## Avoid

- Generic jungle waterfall unrelated to Jeju
- Fantasy glowing waterfall
- Photorealistic landscape painting
- Text labels, signs, UI overlays, or tourism-poster typography
- Overcrowded center that makes the waterfall hard to target

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain viewpoint, walking effort, weather impact, and waterfall cluster choices.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve right-side foliage/sky or lower calm water for the collapsed panel. Do not cover the main waterfall column.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art close-up of Jeju waterfalls, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with the Jeju PixelFlip world, vertical white waterfall streams over dark volcanic basalt rock, lush subtropical greenery, misty pool, small viewing path and tiny visitors for scale, polished professional presentation background, charming mature travel atlas mood, clear hotspot-ready waterfall and viewpoint zones, calm right-side UI safe zone, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Generic tropical jungle, fantasy glowing waterfall, photorealistic painting, text labels, warning signs, UI panels, tourism poster, crude 8-bit graphics, overly dark cave, cluttered foliage hiding the waterfall.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Waterfall silhouette is readable at presentation distance
- Scene adds lush contrast while staying in the same Jeju atlas universe
- UI panel does not cover the waterfall
- Visual supports a short explanation of scenic diversity and pacing
