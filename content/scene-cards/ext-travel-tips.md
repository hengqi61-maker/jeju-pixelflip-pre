# Scene Card: ext-travel-tips

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Travel Advice Summary. This page gives practical trip-quality advice without becoming a checklist slide.

## Core Point

Practical expectations protect the quality of the Jeju experience.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand that transport, weather, pacing, booking timing, and walking effort affect the trip as much as attractions do.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel utility-atlas scene with transport, weather, pacing, luggage, rest, and coastal exposure cues integrated into the same Jeju world.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 atlas scene with a readable travel-flow layout. Keep the page calm and useful, with visual advice cues embedded in roads, ferry, weather, and rest areas rather than labels.

## Must Include

- Road, car, bus, ferry, or parking cue
- Weather/wind/wave cue
- Travelers with luggage or walking gear
- Rest point, shelter, or timing cue
- Distance/pacing feeling
- Clear UI safe zone

## Avoid

- Checklist poster
- Warning sign aesthetic
- Text labels, icons, UI panels, traffic app interface
- Overly anxious mood
- Generic airport/travel graphic unrelated to Jeju

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain transport choice, weather backup, walking comfort, and booking/pacing.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve right-side sky/ocean/road-edge negative space. Do not cover primary transport and weather cues.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art Travel Tips scene for Jeju, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with Jeju PixelFlip world, practical travel-flow scene with roads cars bus ferry parking cues, windy coastal weather, travelers with luggage and walking gear, rest points and timing rhythm, polished calm professional presentation background, information-friendly without text, clear right-side UI safe zone, no labels, no checklist, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Checklist poster, warning sign design, traffic app UI, airport infographic, text labels, icons everywhere, weather app UI, anxious emergency mood, photorealism, mobile game interface, crude 8-bit graphics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Advice cues are practical but not text-heavy
- Scene feels like a branch from Travel Advice Summary
- UI panel does not cover the key travel-flow cues
- Presenter can close with useful, reassuring guidance
