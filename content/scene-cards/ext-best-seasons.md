# Scene Card: ext-best-seasons

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Travel Advice Summary. This page explains seasonal tradeoffs without declaring one universal best answer.

## Core Point

The best Jeju season depends on the traveler's desired atmosphere, activity, and tolerance for weather or crowds.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should leave knowing that spring, summer, autumn, and winter create different versions of Jeju.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel seasonal-atlas scene with four mood zones that remain part of one island world, not a calendar infographic.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 island or landscape composition divided subtly by mood, light, vegetation, and weather. Keep zones visually separable but not boxed like a chart.

## Must Include

- Spring blossom or fresh flower cue
- Summer greenery/coastal water cue
- Autumn clear light or warm field cue
- Winter/wind/cloud/citrus cue
- Small travelers for scale and activity hints
- Clear safe zone for panel

## Avoid

- Calendar grid, weather app UI, or infographic poster
- Text labels for seasons
- Four disconnected postcards
- Overly cute stickers
- Generic seasonal montage unrelated to Jeju

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain seasonal tradeoffs, crowd/weather considerations, and activity fit.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve a calm sky/ocean edge or low-detail seasonal zone. Avoid covering the main seasonal contrast.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art Best Seasons scene for Jeju, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with Jeju PixelFlip world, one unified island world with subtle seasonal mood zones, spring blossoms, green summer coast, clear autumn light, windy winter or citrus cues, small travelers and activity hints, charming professional presentation background, no chart boxes, clear safe zone on right side, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Calendar grid, weather app, infographic poster, season labels, four disconnected postcards, stickers, text, UI panels, photorealistic travel brochure, crude 8-bit graphics, cluttered icons.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Seasonal tradeoffs read visually without text
- Scene feels like a branch from Travel Advice Summary
- UI panel does not cover the main seasonal contrast
- Presenter can explain season choice without overloading the audience
