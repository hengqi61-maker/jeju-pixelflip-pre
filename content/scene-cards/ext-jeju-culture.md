# Scene Card: ext-jeju-culture

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Core Sight Map. This page explains cultural symbols as lived landscape details rather than decorative trivia.

## Core Point

Jeju should feel culturally distinct, not only scenically attractive.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should recognize stone walls, dol hareubang, local houses, citrus, and coastal materials as part of the island's everyday identity.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel-art cultural village scene with dol hareubang, stone walls, low houses, citrus trees, coastal wind, and small everyday-life details.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 isometric village/culture cluster. Keep symbols embedded in a believable miniature world rather than isolated icons. Reserve a calm side area for UI.

## Must Include

- Dol hareubang or stone-grandfather cues
- Basalt stone walls and low island houses
- Citrus trees or field texture
- Small visitors or locals for scale
- Coastal wind/cloud cue or sea edge
- Hotspot-ready zones for stone symbols, village texture, and local identity

## Avoid

- Museum display case feeling
- Generic Korean village with no Jeju identity
- Symbol collage or sticker layout
- Text labels, captions, UI panels, or readable signs
- Childish mascot style

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain dol hareubang, stone walls, citrus, and local living texture.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve a low-detail sky, road, or field edge for the content panel. Do not cover the primary dol hareubang or stone-wall cluster.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art Jeju culture scene, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with the Jeju PixelFlip world, basalt stone walls, dol hareubang stone figures, low island houses, citrus trees, coastal wind cues, small visitors and local everyday details, polished professional presentation background, charming mature cultural atmosphere, clear hotspot-ready cultural symbol zones, calm right-side UI safe zone, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Museum display, generic Korean village, sticker collage, mascot style, readable signs, text labels, tourism poster, photorealism, mobile game village UI, fantasy village, crude 8-bit graphics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Cultural details feel lived-in and spatial, not decorative only
- Scene remains cohesive with the Jeju atlas world
- UI panel does not cover the key cultural symbols
- Presenter can explain identity without turning the page into a lecture
