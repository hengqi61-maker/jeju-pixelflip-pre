# Scene Card: ext-udo

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Core Sight Map. This page gives Udo its own slower satellite-island rhythm after the audience has seen it as a smaller island object in the Jeju atlas.

## Core Point

Udo is not just another stop; it changes the tempo of the trip.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand Udo as a short ferry-linked world-within-the-world, best explained through coastline movement, bikes, open fields, and a gentler pace.

## Visual Intent

A close-up premium pixel atlas scene of Udo as a compact satellite island with ring-road circulation, ferry arrival hints, beaches, fields, scooters or bikes, small boats, and open sea.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 isometric 2.5D view that feels like the camera has moved into the small island seen near Jeju's east coast. Keep the island readable as a compact loop, with calm ocean space available for UI overlay.

## Must Include

- Udo as a small satellite island surrounded by bright coastal water
- Ferry or harbor cue connecting it back to Jeju
- Ring road or coastal path
- Bikes, scooters, small cars, or tiny visitors for scale
- Open fields, low hills, beaches, and village clusters
- Clear hotspot-ready visual zones for ferry/arrival, coast loop, and slow island rhythm

## Avoid

- Generic tropical island unrelated to Jeju
- Overbuilt resort feeling
- Dense city layout
- Text, labels, signs, UI overlays, or route numbers
- Photorealism, poster composition, mobile game quest-map style

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can keep this scene as a terminal branch with return controls. Optional later hotspots may explain ferry timing, bike loop pacing, and coastline stops.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve the right or upper-right ocean area for the collapsed content panel. Keep the island loop and harbor cue away from the panel.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art close-up of Udo island near Jeju, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with the Jeju PixelFlip visual world, compact satellite island surrounded by turquoise coastal water, small ferry harbor, ring road, bikes and scooters, open fields, beaches, low hills, village clusters, tiny travelers, calm professional presentation background, clear explorable miniature world, right-side ocean safe zone for UI, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Generic tropical resort, fantasy island, mobile game UI, quest markers, text labels, route numbers, photorealistic map, tourism poster, crowded beach poster, crude 8-bit graphics, blurry painterly texture, unrelated architecture.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Feels like a zoom into the Udo anchor from the parent Jeju map
- Communicates slower rhythm through visual structure rather than text
- UI panel does not cover harbor, island loop, or main coastline
- Works as a prepared explanation page even without nested hotspots
