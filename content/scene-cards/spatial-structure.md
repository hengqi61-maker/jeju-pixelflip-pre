# Scene Card: spatial-structure

## Scene Role

Mainline geography explanation scene. This scene teaches the audience how Jeju's central mountain, coastal ring, regional differences, and movement logic shape travel planning.

## Core Point

Jeju travel makes sense once the audience understands the island as a center-periphery system.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand why Hallasan, coastal circulation, east-coast landmarks, southern scenery, and geological coast zones naturally create travel clusters.

## Visual Intent

A structured pixel-art atlas scene that preserves miniature-world charm while making Jeju's spatial logic clear. It should feel more explanatory than the cover, but still scenic and immersive.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 isometric or near-isometric island layout. Hallasan should sit clearly in the center. Coastal road or movement logic should wrap around the island. Region zones should be visible through terrain, color, route traces, or clustered visual cues. Keep the central mountain visible even with the UI panel.

## Must Include

- Hallasan as central mountain core
- Clear island-wide center-periphery relationship
- Coastal ring-road or movement logic
- East coast zone with Seongsan and Udo relationship
- South scenic belt with waterfall or lush cue
- Geological coast cue with cliffs or basalt terrain
- Visual separation between mountain, coast, east, south, and geological zones

## Avoid

- Pure infographic map with no world feeling
- Random attraction collage
- Overly decorative details that hide structure
- Dominant route lines that overpower geography
- Long baked-in labels
- Realistic satellite-map look
- Generic island diagram

## Hotspot Plan

Planned region hotspots:

- Central mountain core
- East coast
- Udo link
- Geological coast
- South scenic belt

Hotspot regions should be broad enough for region-based annotation and visually distinct enough to explain without labels.

## UI Safe Zones

- Content panel safe zone: right or lower-right side
- Hallasan must remain uncovered
- Breadcrumb safe zone: top edge, away from route/region emphasis
- Route traces should remain readable under the hotspot overlay

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art atlas of Jeju Island spatial structure, 16:9 isometric 2.5D miniature world, central Hallasan mountain as dominant geographic anchor, clear center-periphery island structure, coastal ring road and movement logic, east coast zone with Seongsan Ilchulbong and Udo ferry relationship, south scenic belt with waterfall and lush terrain, west or southwest geological coast with basalt cliffs, restrained region separation using terrain color and route traces, polished presentation-friendly travel atlas, professional and charming, clear UI safe area, no long text, no labels, no game HUD.

## Negative Prompt

Flat infographic, plain route map, mobile strategy game map, tourism poster, satellite map, photorealistic terrain, excessive arrows, excessive labels, childish icons, fantasy map, crowded pins, UI buttons, title text, crude 8-bit pixels, over-saturated colors.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Hallasan-centered structure is clear at a glance
- Coastal movement logic is visible but not overpowering
- Five planned region hotspots can be annotated cleanly
- UI panel does not cover Hallasan
- Scene feels explanatory and explorable, not like a flat diagram
