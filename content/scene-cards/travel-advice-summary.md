# Scene Card: travel-advice-summary

## Scene Role

Fifth and final mainline scene. This page closes the prepared presentation by turning the atlas into practical judgment: season, pacing, transport, weather, and expectation-setting.

## Core Point

Season, pacing, and weather awareness shape a successful Jeju trip as much as the landmarks do.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should leave with a clear sense of how to choose a travel season, pace the route, and avoid common friction points in Jeju.

## Visual Intent

A calmer premium pixel travel-atlas summary scene with practical travel cues. It should feel like the closing page of the same Jeju visual world, not a list of tips or an infographic slide.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 atlas composition with Jeju as a softened miniature world and several practical cue zones. The image can be less dense than the hub map. It should create a closing mood while still supporting hotspots for Best Seasons and Travel Tips.

## Must Include

- Jeju island or recognizable island fragments
- Seasonal mood cues such as spring blossom, green summer coast, clear autumn light, wind or winter atmosphere
- Transport or pacing cues such as roads, cars, bus stops, ferry, luggage, or walking paths
- Weather awareness cues such as wind, clouds, waves, or coastal exposure
- Small travelers to show scale and comfort
- A visual sense of summary and decision-making
- Calm space for UI overlay

## Avoid

- Text-heavy travel tip poster
- Checklist infographic layout
- Generic calendar graphic
- Weather app interface
- Overly decorative seasonal collage
- Baked-in labels, long text, icons, or UI panels
- Sad or warning-heavy mood

## Hotspot Plan

Planned hotspots:

- Best Seasons
- Travel Tips

The two hotspot regions should feel like visual advice zones: one seasonal/atmospheric, one practical/transport-oriented.

## UI Safe Zones

- Content panel safe zone: right side or upper-right, preferably over calm sky or ocean
- Breadcrumb safe zone: top edge with low detail
- Keep seasonal and transport cue zones clear enough for hover outlines and tooltips
- Avoid placing both hotspots under the same panel area

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art closing advice scene for Jeju Island, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with existing Jeju PixelFlip scenes, calm miniature island world with practical travel cues, seasonal atmosphere zones with blossom greenery clear light wind and coastal weather, transport and pacing cues with roads small cars ferry hints walking travelers and luggage, polished professional presentation background, charming but mature, information-friendly pixel art, clear hotspot regions for best seasons and travel tips, calm right-side UI safe zone, no text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Checklist poster, travel infographic, calendar UI, weather app UI, text labels, long written tips, generic tourism brochure, photorealistic scene, flat icon map, mobile game screen, childish stickers, overloaded symbols, warning sign aesthetic, crude 8-bit graphics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- The scene feels like a closing summary, not a new unrelated page
- Best Seasons and Travel Tips zones are visually separable
- Practical advice is communicated through visual cues, not embedded text
- UI panel does not obscure the two main hotspot regions
- Presenter can use the scene to close the talk with actionable guidance
