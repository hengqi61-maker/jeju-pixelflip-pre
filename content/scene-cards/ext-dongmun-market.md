# Scene Card: ext-dongmun-market

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Core Sight Map. This page gives the atlas an urban, social, food-and-produce contrast to nature-heavy branches.

## Core Point

Dongmun Market condenses Jeju's local energy into a dense, walkable node.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand Dongmun Market as a practical and atmospheric place where food, produce, gifts, night-market energy, and city movement overlap.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel-art market district with roof rhythm, stalls, warm lights, seafood and citrus cues, narrow pedestrian lanes, small crowds, and city-island texture.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 isometric market block or street cluster. Keep aisles and stall rows readable, with a calmer edge for UI overlay. The scene should feel dense but not chaotic.

## Must Include

- Market roofs, stalls, or covered lanes
- Seafood, citrus, snack, or produce cues without text labels
- Warm stall lighting and pedestrian movement
- Small visitors and vendors for scale
- Urban Jeju context: street edge, harbor/city hint, or nearby roads
- Hotspot-ready zones for food stalls, produce, and walking route

## Avoid

- Generic Asian market unrelated to Jeju
- Food collage without spatial layout
- Text-heavy shop signs or readable labels
- Nightclub/neon overload
- Photorealistic street scene or mobile game market screen

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain market timing, snacks/seafood, produce, and relation to the Food branch.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve upper-right roof/sky/street edge or a low-detail side lane. Do not cover the main stall rhythm.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art close-up of Dongmun Market in Jeju, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with the Jeju PixelFlip world, dense but readable market district with roof rhythm, produce and citrus stalls, seafood cues, warm stall lights, narrow pedestrian lanes, tiny visitors and vendors, city-island atmosphere, polished professional presentation background, clear hotspot-ready stall and walking zones, calm right-side UI safe zone, no readable text, no labels, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Generic market, food collage, readable shop signs, heavy text, neon overload, night club mood, mobile game UI, photorealistic street photo, tourism poster, crude 8-bit art, chaotic crowd, blurry painterly textures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Market density is readable and not visually noisy
- Scene feels connected to Jeju food/culture rather than a generic city market
- UI panel does not cover main stall or aisle structure
- Presenter can contrast urban/local energy with nature-heavy scenes
