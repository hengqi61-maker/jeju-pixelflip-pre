# Scene Card: ext-three-day-route

## Scene Role

Extension scene from Recommended Routes. This page explains a balanced three-day itinerary.

## Core Point

Three days lets Jeju breathe: east, central, coastal, food, and city experiences can be grouped without constant rushing.

## Audience Takeaway

The audience should understand how day-based grouping turns the atlas into a usable itinerary.

## Visual Intent

A premium pixel route-atlas scene with three visually distinct day clusters and a coherent travel rhythm across Jeju.

## Composition

Use a 16:9 island-atlas view or slightly zoomed map. Separate the three day clusters through color temperature, route arcs, or spatial grouping without text labels.

## Must Include

- Three visually distinct route clusters
- East-coast icon cluster, central/geographic cluster, and city/food/coastal cluster
- Roads, cars, ferry hints, rest points, or lodging cue
- Balanced route arcs that do not overpower the world
- Clear UI safe zone

## Avoid

- Numbered day labels baked into art
- Overcrowded full-island checklist
- Subway-map style or GPS app look
- Text labels, pins everywhere, UI panels
- Route lines that hide landmarks

## Hotspot Plan

Phase one can stay terminal. Optional later hotspots may explain day grouping, pacing, and alternate weather choices.

## UI Safe Zones

Reserve upper-right or ocean-side negative space. Keep day clusters away from the content panel.

## Art Prompt

Premium medium-resolution pixel art three-day Jeju route scene, 16:9 isometric 2.5D travel atlas style, cohesive with Jeju PixelFlip world, recognizable island map with three balanced route clusters, east-coast landmark rhythm, central Hallasan geography, city food and coastal experiences, elegant route arcs, small cars ferry hints rest points and lodging cues, polished professional presentation background, clear spatial grouping, calm right-side UI safe zone, no text, no labels, no numbers, no UI chrome.

## Negative Prompt

Numbered itinerary poster, GPS app, subway diagram, flat transit map, text labels, legend blocks, crowded pins, mobile game quest map, photorealistic satellite image, crude 8-bit art.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scores at least 40/50 using `ART_DIRECTION_QA.md`
- Three-day rhythm is readable without labels
- Scene feels like a branch from Recommended Routes
- UI panel does not obscure major clusters
- Presenter can explain route grouping and pacing clearly
